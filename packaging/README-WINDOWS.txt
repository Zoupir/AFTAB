AFTAB RESEARCH - Windows GUI preview

Run aftab-windows-amd64.exe from this folder.
The app opens a browser-based control panel on your own computer.

Select your language, enter the server address, your account token
and the pinned certificate SHA-256 fingerprint, then Save, Test, Connect.
The proxy runs locally at 127.0.0.1:10808 when connected.

IMPORTANT: This is not an operating-system VPN. Configure your
application to use SOCKS5 explicitly. It is experimental software.

Do not share your account token or configuration file.
More: https://github.com/Zoupir/AFTAB/blob/main/docs/DESKTOP-CLIENT.md
